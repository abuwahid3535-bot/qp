Sri S. Madhusudhana Rao is the Principal of Mother Theresa Degree College (MTDC), Palamaner. He is an experienced educationist with around 25 years of experience in the field of education. He holds an M.Sc. in Mathematics and an M.Phil. from M.K. University, along with a B.Ed. His academic background and extensive experience reflect his commitment to education and continuous learning. As Principal, he provides academic and administrative leadership to the institution and works towards creating a supportive environment for students and faculty.
